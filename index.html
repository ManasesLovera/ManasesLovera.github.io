01 Approach

Make it work. Then make it cheaper.

  1. Benchmarked on your data. Sometimes it's an LLM, sometimes classic ML that costs almost nothing.

  2. Log inputs, outputs and corrections from day one. Today's API calls become tomorrow's training set.

  3. Compared with evals and A/B tests before anything ships.

  4. Fine-tune a small LM, a VLM or a YOLO detector, or route the easy cases away from the expensive model.

  5. A hosted API, local inference with Ollama or vLLM, or a container beside your data.
